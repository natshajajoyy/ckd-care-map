ACEI and ARB in CKD

Starting and maximum doses, the adjustment for reduced kidney function, and what to do with the creatinine and potassium you check afterwards.

Check Cr and K within 2–4 weeks of starting or increasing the dose.

Creatinine rise after starting or titrating
Rise in CrWhat to do with the ACEI / ARB
< 30%Continue — the dose may be increased up to the maximum
30 – 50%Reduce the dose by 50% if no other cause is found, and consider increasing it again once the Cr has fallen
> 50%Stop the drug if no other cause is found; consider restarting at a low dose once the Cr has fallen
Always look for another cause first
  • Volume depletion or heart failure
  • NSAIDs or herbal medicines
  • Renal artery stenosis — very rare; order doppler ultrasound of the renal arteries only where there is an indication for angioplasty, such as heart failure or a rapidly progressive eGFR decline

The drug itself

Do not reduce or stop the ACEI / ARB.

Except K ≥ 6.0 mEq/L — hold the drug, lower the potassium first, then consider restarting once K is below 5.0 mEq/L.
