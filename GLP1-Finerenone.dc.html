GLP-1RA and finerenone in diabetic kidney disease

When to consider adding either agent on top of ACEI/ARB and SGLT2i therapy.

Long-acting GLP-1RA
  • Blood glucose not at target after metformin + SGLT2i, especially with ASCVD or obesity, or
  • Metformin or SGLT2i cannot be used, or
  • Residual albuminuria remains high
Finerenone
  • eGFR > 25 ml/min/1.73 m² and residual UACR > 30 mg/gCr
  • After maximum tolerated dose of ACEI/ARB ± SGLT2i
  • K ≤ 4.8 mEq/L

Adapted from Thai CKD 2022, KDIGO CKD 2024.
