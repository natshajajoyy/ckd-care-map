Sheet 03 Advice

What to advise at every visit

Four headings to cover with every patient with CKD: nutrition, behaviour, drug safety and vaccination.

01  Nutrition
  • Restrict salt and salty food
  • Protein
    • Non-dialysis: protein restriction
    • Dialysis: normal protein intake
Nutrition knowledge →

Sodium, protein servings, potassium and phosphorus food lists. Patient handout — link to be added

02  Behaviour
  • Stop smoking
  • Exercise ≥ 150 minutes per week — 30 minutes a day, 5 days a week
  • Body mass index 18.5 – 22.9 kg/m² — reduce weight if above range
03  Drug safety
Avoid

NSAIDs and herbal medicines

Hold temporarily on sick days
ACEI / ARB SGLT2i Metformin Diuretics
04  Vaccination
Hepatitis B — if HBsAg negative and anti-HBs negative
G1–220 mcg at 0, 1, 6 months — 3 doses
G3–420 mcg at 0, 1, 2, 6 months — 4 doses
G540 mcg at 0, 1, 2, 6 months — double dose, 8 vials

Check anti-HBs one month after the last dose.

PneumococcalPCV20 × 1 dose
