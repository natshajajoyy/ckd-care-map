Sheet 01 Diagnosis & staging

Chronic kidney disease

A teaching care map for the outpatient department: definition and staging, monitoring, advice, and the treatment of complications and comorbidities.

Definition

Abnormality of kidney structure or function present for more than 3 months, with either:

  • eGFR < 60 ml/min/1.73 m², or
  • a marker of kidney damage — albuminuria, UACR ≥ 30 mg/g (≥ A2)

Indications for ultrasound KUB
  1. Microscopic or gross hematuria — rule out renal stone
  2. Recurrent UTI
  3. Suspected obstructive uropathy — flank pain, history of stone, intra-abdominal or GU malignancy
  4. Rapidly progressive CKD — ± doppler to rule out renal artery stenosis
  5. Family history of ADPKD, or positive bimanual palpation

Not every patient with CKD needs an ultrasound KUB.
