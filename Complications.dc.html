Sheet 04 Complications

Treating the complications of CKD

Pick a complication, then work down the tree one decision at a time.

These complications begin to appear in CKD G3b and are common in CKD G4.

Goals at a glance
Anemia
Correct iron deficiency first
ESA by nephrology if renal anemia persists
Hyperkalemia
K < 5.5 mEq/L
CKD-MBD
Ca, P, PTH to target
Vitamin D > 30 ng/ml — see table below
Metabolic acidosis
HCO₃ 24–26 mEq/L
Start treatment if HCO₃ < 22 mEq/L
